GRAVY HEIST: the turkey stole the farmer's gravy boat — outrun the farmer's family, grease the floor, catch the getaway goose. Gravy is BOTH your health and your money: hits cost gravy, drips and corn refill it, toll gates take a 15-gravy bribe (or a clean jump over them), and spilling grease sends Farmer Jed, Biscuit the dog and the twins with nets sliding into the pond. Three farm zones — the Barnyard, the Corn Maze, Creek Road — and a getaway goose waiting at the end of the creek. Seeded runs (`?seed=42`), WebAudio synth sound, keyboard + mouse controls, win/lose/score, start and restart.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network requests, plays offline in a desktop browser.

Controls: SPACE jump · ↓ slide under the corn · G spill grease (8 gravy) · B pay the toll gate (15 gravy) · P pause · M mute · R restart.
